# Product Discovery Document

**Project:** FlowDesk

**Client:** Zenith Retail Group

**Prepared By:** Development Team

**Document Status:** Draft v1.0

**Source:** Discovery Meetings DM-01 to DM-04

---

# 1. Executive Summary

FlowDesk is an internal operations platform designed for Zenith Retail Group to replace paper-based operational processes with a centralized digital system.

The discovery process identified attendance management, leave management, and operational visibility as the organization's highest-priority challenges. These processes currently rely on paper records, spreadsheets, WhatsApp communication, and manual data entry, leading to operational inefficiencies, payroll disputes, and limited visibility across branches.

The proposed first release focuses on solving these operational pain points through a phased approach, prioritizing business value over feature quantity.

---

# 2. Business Overview

Zenith Retail Group operates six retail branches supported by a small Head Office.

Daily operations are primarily managed at branch level while HR, payroll preparation, and major approvals are coordinated centrally.

As the organization has grown, operational processes have remained largely manual, creating increasing administrative overhead and reducing visibility across the business.

---

# 3. Business Problems

The discovery process identified the following primary operational challenges.

## Attendance

Attendance is currently recorded manually using paper registers before being transferred multiple times into payroll spreadsheets.

This creates:

- Duplicate work
- Human error
- Payroll disputes
- Delayed processing

---

## Leave Management

Leave requests are handled verbally or on paper.

Approval history is inconsistent and difficult to trace.

Employees often have no visibility into request status.

---

## Asset Management

Company assets are tracked inconsistently across branches.

The organization lacks reliable accountability for asset assignments, returns, and condition.

---

## Operational Visibility

Head Office lacks real-time visibility into branch operations.

Managers spend significant time manually collecting operational information.

---

# 4. Project Vision

FlowDesk aims to become the organization's centralized operational workspace where employees, branch managers, and Head Office can complete day-to-day operational tasks using a single trusted system.

The platform should reduce administrative effort while increasing transparency, accountability, and operational visibility.

---

# 5. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Digitize attendance management
- Digitize leave management
- Improve operational transparency
- Reduce manual administrative work
- Eliminate duplicate data entry
- Improve accountability
- Reduce payroll disputes
- Provide operational visibility across branches

# 8. Proposed MVP Scope

The first release should prioritize solving the organization's highest-value operational problems.

### Included

- Attendance Management
- Leave Management
- Notifications
- Approval Workflow
- Operational Dashboard
- Audit History

### Deferred

- Complete Asset Management
- Advanced Reporting
- Additional HR Processes
- Payroll Integration

Asset Management should be designed from the beginning but implemented in a later phase unless required during MVP development.

---

# 9. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it achieves the following outcomes.

- Employees can submit and track leave digitally.
- Attendance no longer requires multiple manual data transfers.
- Managers can approve requests through a standardized workflow.
- Head Office gains visibility into attendance and pending approvals.
- Actions affecting payroll or approvals become traceable through audit history.
- Operational disputes are reduced through improved transparency.

---

# 10. Constraints

The client identified the following project constraints.

- Budget is limited.
- Delivery should follow a phased approach.
- Attendance and Leave are the highest-priority modules.
- Solutions should prioritize practical business value over unnecessary complexity.

---

# 11. Out of Scope (Initial Release)

The following items are intentionally excluded from the first release.

- Payroll automation
- Recruitment
- Performance management
- Employee evaluation
- Finance modules
- Inventory management

These may be considered for future phases if business priorities change.

---

# 12. Discovery Conclusion

The discovery phase established a shared understanding of the client's operational challenges, business goals, and desired outcomes.
