# Business Rules (Validated)
- Attendance is recorded manually using a physical register.
- Attendance is summarized weekly by the Branch Manager.
- HR manually consolidates attendance for payroll.
- Leave up to approximately 2–3 days can be approved at branch level.
- Longer leave requires Head Office approval.
- Three late arrivals are intended to trigger a warning, although this is not consistently enforced.
- Emergency leave may be approved verbally before documentation.
- Shift swaps occur informally and require manager awareness but have no standardized process.
